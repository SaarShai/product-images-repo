# Prompt V5 - Safe Margins And Top Bridge

Use the uploaded watercolor castle reference for style. Use the uploaded
two-panel template/SVG preview only as a production constraint map.

Generate artwork only. The exact SVG dieline, center slot, horizontal divide,
notches, safe-area crop, and exclusion rectangles will be applied afterward.
Do not draw any black cut line, dashed guide, red rectangle, yellow safe-area
line, green contour line, label, measurement, annotation, or production guide.

## Style

Match the reference closely: delicate children's-book watercolor, ivory and
cream stone, terracotta/coral roofs, pale gold glowing windows, soft blue-grey
shadows, hand-painted masonry, muted sage foliage, blush and peach flowers,
tiny birds, butterflies, a few fairies, gentle magical glow, airy white
background.

## Composition

Create a tall fairytale castle garden built around an intentional open center.
The protected center should feel like a designed courtyard/light-well gap
between two castle wings, not like artwork that was faded, erased, blurred, or
washed away afterward.

- Build the castle as two vertical side clusters, one left and one right.
- Keep the side clusters narrower than before so all artwork stays comfortably
  inside the future yellow safe-area margin.
- Leave a clean white border along both side edges and the bottom edge.
- Do not let towers, foliage, flowers, birds, fairies, wings, roof tips,
  balconies, flags, or decorative motifs touch or cross the outer margin.
- Leave the central vertical corridor blank from top to bottom as a designed
  open air passage between the two castle wings.
- Do not place a main castle door in the lower center.
- If a door appears at all, move it clearly to the left or right side and keep
  it away from the center corridor.
- Keep the lower center as an intentionally open walkway/courtyard gap with
  crisp surrounding garden and stone edges on the left and right.

## Top Reconnection

The left and right tower groups should reconnect near the top with a high,
graceful castle element that sits above the short upper center no-feature
rectangle.

Use one broad, production-friendly architectural connector, such as:

- a high arched stone skybridge,
- a crenellated parapet bridge,
- a delicate elevated castle wall,
- or a broad balcony/terrace spanning between the upper towers.

The connector should feel structurally part of the castle, not a random band.
It may curve or arch over the open center, but it must not contain tiny focal
details inside the short upper red keep-out zone. The connector should sit high
enough that the short upper keep-out rectangle below it remains clean, and the
vertical slot corridor below remains open.

Avoid placing birds, butterflies, fairies, flags, roof tips, faces, windows, or
flower clusters in the top keep-out rectangle. The high connector may define
the broad top silhouette, but the keep-out rectangle itself should remain clear.

## Clearance Bands

Reserve these areas as intentional open production spaces:

- Central vertical lane: the middle 20 to 25 percent of the image width, from
  top to bottom, should contain no focal elements.
- Center slot buffer: shape the castle walls, towers, balconies, vines, and
  flower beds so they stop cleanly before the center lane.
- Middle horizontal band: a quiet band across the image around the panel split,
  roughly just above the visual midpoint, should contain no windows, doors,
  faces, birds, butterflies, roof tips, flags, flower heads, or fairy bodies.
- Lower center keep-clear: no door, flower cluster, lamp, window, character,
  focal object, or decorative motif in the lower-center vertical zone.
- Outer safe margin: all artwork must sit inside the future yellow dashed safe
  area; the area between the safe area and final cut line should be clean white
  space.

Quiet zones should look like intentional open space in the design: clean white
air, empty courtyard, or a narrow unoccupied passage. Do not use a fade effect,
blurred wash, transparency, ghosted detail, smeared texture, fog, pale wash, or
erased-looking artwork to create these spaces. The artwork must be built around
these open spaces.

## Top Silhouette

The top should remain strong and easy to trace later:

- Use broad towers, roof shapes, and the high connector to create a graceful
  castle skyline.
- Avoid tiny birds, butterflies, fairies, flag tips, or loose sparkles above
  the major roof/tower/bridge silhouette.
- Keep the highest shapes production-friendly: large, simple, and clean.

## Negative Instructions

Do not create a new die-line. Do not place a centered castle entrance, centered
window stack, centered fairy, centered flower bouquet, or centered path
decoration. Do not fill the middle split band with tower detail. Do not let the
side clusters expand into the outer safe margin. Do not make the center look
faded out, airbrushed, erased, foggy, hidden by pale wash, or removed after the
fact.

## Output

Return a high-resolution vertical watercolor artwork on white background,
roughly matching the tall proportions of the supplied template. It should be
beautiful alone, but conservative enough that the exact SVG can be overlaid
without cutting through important details.
