# Prompt V3 - SVG Mask Artwork Only

Use the uploaded watercolor castle reference for style and the uploaded
two-panel template/SVG preview only as a production constraint map.

Create a clean watercolor fairytale castle illustration that is designed to be
masked later by the exact SVG dieline. Do not draw the final dieline yourself.
Do not redraw black cut lines, yellow dashed safe-area lines, red exclusion
rectangles, green contour guides, labels, measurements, or annotations.

The output should be artwork only on a white background.

## Style Target

Match the reference image closely:

- Delicate children's-book watercolor.
- Warm ivory and cream stone towers.
- Terracotta, coral, and peach castle roofs.
- Pale gold glowing windows.
- Soft blue-grey shadows and hand-painted stone texture.
- Muted sage greenery, blush flowers, tiny birds, butterflies, fairies, and
  subtle magical glow.
- Airy white background with no heavy digital rendering.

## Composition Target

Make one tall, continuous castle scene that can sit behind the exact SVG
template. Use large simple castle masses first, then secondary detail.

The illustration should have:

- Upper half: the main castle skyline, varied tower heights, round towers,
  spires, parapets, and a graceful top silhouette.
- Lower half: calmer stone walls, a central castle-door area, vines and flowers,
  with fewer tall focal elements than the top.
- Left and right halves: visually balanced, but not mirrored.
- Main towers and roofs large enough to read after cutting.

## Hard Blank Zones

Leave these regions visually quiet, pale, and free of focal details because the
exact SVG slot, fold/divide, and no-feature guides will be applied later:

- A full-height vertical blank corridor through the center of the artwork.
- Extra blank buffer on both sides of that corridor.
- A horizontal quiet band through the middle where the two panels meet.
- Small no-feature zones above and below the center slot.
- A clean outer margin around every future cut edge.

No fairies, faces, birds, butterflies, roof tips, flags, windows, doors,
flowers, or decorative focal motifs should enter those blank zones. Those zones
may contain only white background, very pale stone wash, or barely visible
generic texture.

## Top Silhouette

The top skyline should be strong and production-friendly:

- Use only broad tower and roof shapes near the top edge.
- Avoid tiny floating details above the towers.
- Keep birds, butterflies, sparkles, and fairies below the major skyline.
- Let the later SVG contour trace the castle silhouette without needing small
  bumps for tiny details.

## Negative Instructions

Do not create a new die-line shape. Do not alter the two-panel template. Do not
make the lower panel wider, shorter, rounded, arched, or decorative. Do not add
visible guide lines. Do not put important details across the center slot or the
middle divide. Do not place characters near cut/fold/slot edges.

## Output

Return a high-resolution vertical watercolor artwork on white background,
roughly matching the tall proportions of the supplied template. It should look
beautiful by itself, but it must be conservative enough that the exact SVG
template can be overlaid afterward without cutting through important details.
