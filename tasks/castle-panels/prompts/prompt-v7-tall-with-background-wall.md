# Prompt V7 - Taller Artwork With Background Wall

Use the uploaded watercolor castle reference for style. Use the uploaded
two-panel template/SVG preview only as a production constraint map.

Generate artwork only. The exact SVG dieline, center slot, horizontal divide,
notches, safe-area crop, and exclusion rectangles will be applied afterward.
Do not draw any black cut line, dashed guide, red rectangle, yellow safe-area
line, green contour line, label, measurement, annotation, or production guide.

## What V7 Fixes

The previous candidate had good side gutters after layout scaling, but:

- The bottom of the illustration sat too far above the bottom yellow safe area.
- The artwork could be taller, with the castle rising about one top-spire
  height higher.
- The middle keep-clear region was too empty/white. It should contain a plain
  castle-wall background element, not a focal detail.

Known correction from review: the horizontal top-bottom dividing line must not
cut through fairies, birds, butterflies, wings, flowers, windows, lamps, roof
tips, or other recognizable motifs. In the reviewed V7 result, a right-side
fairy was crossed by this line. Treat the horizontal split as a hard
no-focal-element band.

## Style

Match the reference closely: delicate children's-book watercolor, ivory and
cream stone, terracotta/coral roofs, pale gold glowing windows, soft blue-grey
shadows, hand-painted masonry, muted sage foliage, blush and peach flowers,
tiny birds, butterflies, a few fairies, gentle magical glow, airy white
background.

## Overall Composition

Create a tall fairytale castle garden built around an engineered center lane.
The castle should feel intentionally designed around the slot/no-feature zones,
not faded or erased.

- Make the castle illustration vertically taller than the previous version.
- Let the upper spires and high bridge sit higher, roughly one small spire
  height closer to the top safe area.
- Let the lower flowers/path/garden reach down to the bottom yellow safe area.
- Do not leave a large blank white strip between the bottom of the illustration
  and the bottom safe-area line.
- Keep the artwork comfortably inside the left and right yellow safe-area
  margins; side gutters remain important.
- Keep the high top bridge/parapet reconnecting the left and right castle
  groups above the short upper keep-out rectangle.
- Leave a quiet horizontal split band through the middle of the artwork. The
  split may pass over plain wall, soft path, low foliage wash, or empty
  background only.
- Do not place a fairy, bird, butterfly, wing, face, flower head, window, lamp,
  flag, roof tip, or other recognizable motif on or across the horizontal
  split.

## Middle Background Wall

The center lane and the red-rectangle areas should not be empty white. Fill
them with a quiet background castle-wall surface.

Use:

- simple ivory stone wall texture,
- subtle masonry blocks,
- faint watercolor shading,
- or a plain rear courtyard wall.

Do not place focal elements in this wall area:

- no windows,
- no doors,
- no faces,
- no fairies,
- no birds,
- no butterflies,
- no flower heads,
- no lamps,
- no flags,
- no roof tips,
- no decorative symbols.

The wall should read as background architecture only. It should be safe for the
SVG slot, red rectangles, and horizontal split to pass over it without cutting
through an important motif.

## Center And Split Rules

- The exact central slot/red-rectangle lane may contain only the plain castle
  wall background.
- The middle horizontal split band may cross plain stone wall, quiet path, low
  foliage wash, or empty background only.
- Keep a clear no-focal-element buffer above and below the horizontal split,
  including near the left and right notch shapes.
- No fairies, birds, butterflies, fairy wings, faces, flower heads, windows,
  doors, lamps, flags, roof tips, or decorative symbols may sit on, straddle, or
  be partially cut by the horizontal split.
- Castle towers, balconies, windows, fairies, birds, flowers, and detailed
  decorations may approach the center lane, but should stop before entering the
  slot/red-rectangle keep-clear areas.
- The lower center can show a simple path or wall texture, but no centered door,
  lamp, character, window, or flower bouquet.
- If fairies are included, place them fully above or fully below the split with
  visible clearance. Omit side fairies if they would land near the split.

## Top Reconnection

Reconnect the left and right tower groups near the top with one high, graceful
castle element above the short upper center no-feature rectangle.

Use a broad production-friendly architectural connector, such as a high arched
stone skybridge, a crenellated parapet bridge, a delicate elevated castle wall,
or a broad balcony/terrace spanning between the upper towers.

The connector should feel structurally part of the castle. It may arch over the
center wall area, but it must not place tiny focal details inside the short
upper red keep-out zone. The short upper keep-out rectangle should remain free
of focal details, with only plain wall/air behind it.

## Outer Safe Margin

All artwork must sit inside the future yellow dashed safe-area boundary:

- no foliage spilling past the side safe lines,
- no flowers crossing the side or bottom gutters,
- no wings or birds crossing the side gutters,
- no tower edges touching the final cut margin.

The area between the yellow safe area and final cut line should remain clean
white space.

## Negative Instructions

Do not create a new die-line. Do not leave the center lane completely white. Do
not put focal elements in the center red-rectangle areas. Do not make the middle
look faded out, airbrushed, erased, foggy, transparent, or hidden by pale wash.
Do not push the side castle clusters outward into the safe-margin gutters. Do
not allow the horizontal panel split to cut through any fairy, bird, butterfly,
wing, flower, face, window, lamp, roof tip, flag, or other recognizable motif.

## Output

Return a high-resolution vertical watercolor artwork on white background,
roughly matching the tall proportions of the supplied template. It should be
beautiful alone, but conservative enough that the exact SVG can be overlaid
without cutting through important details.
