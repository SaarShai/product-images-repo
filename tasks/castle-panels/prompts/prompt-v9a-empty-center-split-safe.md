# Prompt V9A - Empty Center With Split-Safe Focal Zones

Use the uploaded watercolor castle reference for style. Use the uploaded
two-panel template/SVG preview only as a production constraint map.

Generate artwork only. The exact SVG dieline, center slot, horizontal divide,
notches, safe-area crop, and exclusion rectangles will be applied afterward.
Do not draw any black cut line, dashed guide, red rectangle, yellow safe-area
line, green contour line, label, measurement, annotation, or production guide.

## Mode

This is the V6-style empty-center mode.

Use this prompt when the middle should remain truly empty. The center slot,
red-rectangle areas, and their immediate buffer should read as intentional
blank white space, open air, or a very plain open courtyard gap. Do not fill
the middle with wall texture.

## What This Fixes

V6 was strong because no birds, butterflies, fairies, or other elements were
cropped by the middle rectangles. Preserve that behavior.

Both V6 and V7 still had elements cut by the horizontal top-bottom dividing
line, especially the right-side fairy. Fix that by treating the horizontal
split as a hard no-focal-element band, just like the center slot and red
rectangles.

## Style

Match the reference closely: delicate children's-book watercolor, ivory and
cream stone, terracotta/coral roofs, pale gold glowing windows, soft blue-grey
shadows, hand-painted masonry, muted sage foliage, blush and peach flowers,
gentle magical glow, airy white background.

## Overall Composition

Create a tall fairytale castle garden built around a narrow engineered empty
center. The castle should feel intentionally designed around the slot and
no-feature zones, not faded or erased.

- Build the castle as two rich side clusters, one left and one right.
- Keep the center lane narrow, closer to V6 than V4 or V5.
- Leave the exact center slot/red-rectangle lane empty and clean.
- Pull the side clusters inward enough to preserve safe side gutters.
- Keep the bottom filled with low, quiet path or garden texture, but avoid
  large focal flowers at the bottom edge.
- Keep the high top bridge/parapet reconnecting the left and right castle
  groups above the short upper keep-out rectangle.

## Hard Cut-Band Rules

The future SVG cut paths may cross only empty space or quiet non-focal
background. Treat these as no-focal-element bands:

- the central vertical slot,
- both red-rectangle keep-clear areas,
- the horizontal top-bottom panel split across the artwork,
- the side and bottom safe-area gutters.

Allowed inside those bands:

- blank white,
- open air,
- very plain courtyard gap,
- soft path wash,
- low grass wash,
- faint watercolor shadow.

Forbidden inside or straddling those bands:

- fairies,
- birds,
- butterflies,
- fairy wings,
- faces,
- flower heads,
- windows,
- doors,
- lamps,
- flags,
- roof tips,
- decorative symbols.

If a fairy, bird, butterfly, flower, window, or lamp is used, place it fully in
one safe interior zone with visible clearance from the center rectangles and
from the horizontal split. Do not let any recognizable motif be partially cut.

## Center And Split Rules

- Keep the exact center slot/red-rectangle lane empty.
- Do not fill the middle lane with wall texture in this version.
- The horizontal split may cross blank space, quiet path, low grass, or plain
  background only.
- Do not place a fairy, wing, bird, butterfly, flower head, window, door, lamp,
  flag, roof tip, face, or decorative symbol on the horizontal split line.
- Castle towers, balconies, windows, fairies, birds, flowers, and detailed
  decorations may approach the center lane, but must stop before entering the
  slot/red-rectangle keep-clear areas.
- The lower center can show a simple open path or courtyard gap, but no centered
  door, lamp, character, window, or flower bouquet.
- Tiny flying elements are optional. If included, keep them in safe upper
  interior areas away from the center lane, horizontal split, and side gutters.

## Side And Bottom Margin Discipline

- No fairies near the left or right yellow safe-area margins.
- No birds or butterflies near the left or right yellow safe-area margins.
- No wings crossing or approaching the side safe-area gutters.
- No large flowers touching the bottom yellow safe-area line.
- Keep lower flowers smaller and set inward from the bottom corners.
- Prefer low grass, pale path stones, and soft non-focal foliage near the
  bottom boundary.

## Negative Instructions

Do not create a new die-line. Do not fill the center lane with wall texture. Do
not place focal elements in the center red-rectangle areas. Do not make the
middle look faded out, airbrushed, erased, foggy, transparent, or hidden by pale
wash. Do not allow the horizontal panel split to cut through any fairy, bird,
butterfly, wing, flower, face, window, lamp, roof tip, flag, or other
recognizable motif.

## Output

Return a high-resolution vertical watercolor artwork on white background,
roughly matching the tall proportions of the supplied template. It should be
beautiful alone, but conservative enough that the exact SVG can be overlaid
without cutting through important details.
