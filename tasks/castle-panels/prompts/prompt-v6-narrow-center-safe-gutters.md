# Prompt V6 - Narrow Center Clearance And Safe Gutters

Use the uploaded watercolor castle reference for style. Use the uploaded
two-panel template/SVG preview only as a production constraint map.

Generate artwork only. The exact SVG dieline, center slot, horizontal divide,
notches, safe-area crop, and exclusion rectangles will be applied afterward.
Do not draw any black cut line, dashed guide, red rectangle, yellow safe-area
line, green contour line, label, measurement, annotation, or production guide.

## What V6 Fixes

The previous version protected the center too much, creating an oversized empty
aisle that pushed the castle wings outward into the side safe margins.

In this version:

- Keep the central clearance narrow: only the real slot/red-rectangle lane plus
  a small buffer.
- Pull the left and right castle wings inward so the artwork stays comfortably
  inside the future yellow dashed safe-area lines.
- Keep clear white gutters outside the artwork on both sides and at the bottom.
- Preserve the high top bridge/parapet reconnection from V5.

## Style

Match the reference closely: delicate children's-book watercolor, ivory and
cream stone, terracotta/coral roofs, pale gold glowing windows, soft blue-grey
shadows, hand-painted masonry, muted sage foliage, blush and peach flowers,
tiny birds, butterflies, a few fairies, gentle magical glow, airy white
background.

## Composition

Create a tall fairytale castle garden built around a narrow engineered center
clearance. The center should feel intentionally designed into the castle layout,
not faded or erased, but it should not become a wide empty canyon.

- Build the castle as two rich side clusters, one left and one right.
- Move both side clusters inward compared with the previous version.
- Leave clear white side gutters outside the artwork, matching the future
  margin between the yellow safe area and the black cut line.
- Keep every tower, roof, balcony, fairy, bird, butterfly, wing, vine, flower,
  and decorative motif inside the future yellow safe-area boundary.
- The central vertical clearance should be narrow, roughly 10 to 14 percent of
  the image width, not 20 to 25 percent.
- The castle walls, towers, gardens, and paths may approach the center lane
  closely, as long as they do not enter the slot/red-rectangle keep-clear lane.
- Do not place a main castle door in the lower center.
- If a door appears, move it clearly to the left or right side and keep it
  outside the center keep-clear lane.
- The lower center may contain a simple open path/courtyard passage, but no
  centered focal object.

## Top Reconnection

Reconnect the left and right tower groups near the top with one high, graceful
castle element above the short upper center no-feature rectangle.

Use a broad production-friendly architectural connector, such as:

- a high arched stone skybridge,
- a crenellated parapet bridge,
- a delicate elevated castle wall,
- or a broad balcony/terrace spanning between the upper towers.

The connector should feel structurally part of the castle. It may arch over the
narrow open center, but it must not place tiny focal details inside the short
upper red keep-out zone. The short upper keep-out rectangle should remain clear,
and the vertical slot corridor below should remain open.

## Clearance Rules

- Center slot lane: keep the exact middle slot/red-rectangle lane free of focal
  elements from top to bottom.
- Center buffer: keep only a small buffer outside that lane; do not create a
  huge blank center.
- Middle horizontal band: keep a quiet band around the panel split, but let the
  castle composition come close to it. No faces, fairies, birds, butterflies,
  roof tips, doors, windows, flower heads, or flags should sit directly on the
  split line or notch areas.
- Lower center keep-clear: no door, lamp, character, flower bouquet, window, or
  decorative focal motif in the lower red keep-out lane.
- Outer safe margin: keep the area outside the future yellow dashed safe-area
  lines clean white. No foliage, flowers, towers, spires, wings, birds, or
  butterflies should spill into the side or bottom gutters.

Quiet protected spaces should look like intentional open air, walkway, or
courtyard geometry. Do not use a fade effect, blur, transparency, ghosted
detail, smeared texture, fog, pale wash, or erased-looking artwork to create
these spaces.

## Top Silhouette

The top should remain strong and easy to trace later:

- Use broad towers, roof shapes, and the high connector to create a graceful
  castle skyline.
- Avoid tiny birds, butterflies, fairies, flag tips, or loose sparkles above
  the major roof/tower/bridge silhouette.
- Keep the highest shapes production-friendly: large, simple, and clean.

## Negative Instructions

Do not create a new die-line. Do not make a wide empty vertical canyon. Do not
push the side castle clusters outward into the safe-margin gutters. Do not
place centered focal elements in the slot lane. Do not make the center look
faded out, airbrushed, erased, foggy, hidden by pale wash, or removed after the
fact.

## Output

Return a high-resolution vertical watercolor artwork on white background,
roughly matching the tall proportions of the supplied template. It should be
beautiful alone, but conservative enough that the exact SVG can be overlaid
without cutting through important details.
