# Prompt V8 - Center Wall With Safer Gutters

Use the uploaded watercolor castle reference for style. Use the uploaded
two-panel template/SVG preview only as a production constraint map.

Generate artwork only. The exact SVG dieline, center slot, horizontal divide,
notches, safe-area crop, and exclusion rectangles will be applied afterward.
Do not draw any black cut line, dashed guide, red rectangle, yellow safe-area
line, green contour line, label, measurement, annotation, or production guide.

## What V8 Fixes

V7 proved that the center lane should be filled with quiet ivory castle wall,
not empty white. V7 also became too crowded near the side and bottom safe
margins.

User feedback confirmed that V6 and V7 are both useful modes:

- V6 is the empty-center mode, for designs where the middle should remain
  truly blank.
- V7 is the no-elements-background mode, for designs where the middle should
  contain only plain wall or other inert background.

V8 should use the V7 center-wall mode, return to V6-level margin discipline,
and fix the two newest crop problems: V7's bird and butterfly were cropped by
the middle rectangles, and both V6 and V7 had a right-side fairy crossed by the
horizontal top-bottom dividing line.

## Style

Match the reference closely: delicate children's-book watercolor, ivory and
cream stone, terracotta/coral roofs, pale gold glowing windows, soft blue-grey
shadows, hand-painted masonry, muted sage foliage, blush and peach flowers,
gentle magical glow, airy white background.

## Overall Composition

Create a tall fairytale castle garden built around a narrow engineered center
lane. The castle should feel intentionally designed around the slot/no-feature
zones, not faded or erased.

- Keep the artwork comfortably inside the future yellow safe-area margins.
- Preserve clean white gutters outside the safe-area boundary on both sides and
  along the bottom.
- Let the lower garden/path reach near the bottom safe area, but keep the bottom
  edge low-detail and non-focal.
- Keep the high top bridge/parapet reconnecting the left and right castle
  groups above the short upper keep-out rectangle.
- Keep the side castle masses narrower than V7 so the whole composition has
  breathing room inside the side safe margins.
- Compose the illustration as if every future cut path is already visible:
  focal elements should sit clearly between cut bands, never straddling them.
- Give the horizontal top-bottom split its own quiet clearance band across the
  full width, including the left and right notch areas.

## Center Background Wall

Fill the center lane and red-rectangle areas with quiet background castle-wall
surface.

Use:

- simple ivory stone wall texture,
- subtle masonry blocks,
- faint watercolor shading,
- or a plain rear courtyard wall.

This wall should be background architecture only. It must be safe for the SVG
slot, red rectangles, and horizontal split to pass over it without cutting
through an important motif.

Do not place focal elements in this wall area:

- no windows,
- no doors,
- no faces,
- no fairies,
- no birds,
- no butterflies,
- no flower heads,
- no lamps,
- no flags,
- no roof tips,
- no decorative symbols.

## Cut-Band Hierarchy

This is the strictest production rule.

The future SVG cut paths may cross background only. Treat these as
no-focal-element bands:

- the central vertical slot,
- both red-rectangle keep-clear areas,
- the horizontal top-bottom panel split across the artwork,
- the side and bottom safe-area gutters.

Allowed inside those bands:

- empty white,
- plain ivory wall,
- quiet masonry,
- soft path stones,
- low grass wash,
- faint watercolor shadow.

Forbidden inside or straddling those bands:

- fairies,
- birds,
- butterflies,
- fairy wings,
- faces,
- flower heads,
- windows,
- doors,
- lamps,
- flags,
- roof tips,
- decorative symbols.

If a fairy, bird, butterfly, flower, window, or lamp is used, place it fully in
one safe interior zone with visible clearance from the center rectangles and
from the horizontal split. Do not let any recognizable motif be partially cut.

## Horizontal Split Correction

This directly addresses the reviewed V6/V7 issue where a right-side fairy was
cut by the top-bottom dividing line.

- Leave a quiet, low-detail band across the full horizontal split.
- The split may cross plain wall, quiet path, low grass wash, faint shadow, or
  empty background only.
- Keep both side notch areas free of fairies, birds, butterflies, wings,
  flowers, windows, lamps, flags, roof tips, and decorative symbols.
- Do not place a side fairy with wings near the split. Put any fairy fully
  above or fully below the split with visible clearance, or omit fairies
  entirely.

## Side And Bottom Margin Discipline

This is the most important correction from V7.

- No fairies near the left or right yellow safe-area margins.
- No birds or butterflies near the left or right yellow safe-area margins.
- No wings crossing or approaching the side safe-area gutters.
- No large flowers touching the bottom yellow safe-area line.
- No tall flower stems, vines, leaves, or roof tips pushing into side gutters.
- Keep lower flowers smaller and set inward from the bottom corners.
- Prefer low grass, pale path stones, and soft non-focal foliage near the
  bottom boundary.
- Keep the main decorative flowers, fairies, birds, butterflies, windows,
  lamps, and doors well inside the template, away from gutters and red zones.

## Center And Split Rules

- The exact central slot/red-rectangle lane may contain only the plain castle
  wall background.
- The middle horizontal split band may cross plain stone wall, quiet path, or
  low garden texture, but no focal elements.
- Do not place a fairy, wing, bird, butterfly, flower head, window, door, lamp,
  flag, roof tip, face, or decorative symbol on the horizontal split line.
- Do not let focal elements cross the left or right notch shapes attached to
  the split line.
- Castle towers, balconies, windows, fairies, birds, flowers, and detailed
  decorations may approach the center lane, but must stop before entering the
  slot/red-rectangle keep-clear areas.
- The lower center can show a simple path or wall texture, but no centered door,
  lamp, character, window, or flower bouquet.
- Tiny flying elements are optional. If they are included, keep them in the safe
  upper interior areas, away from the center lane, the horizontal split, and the
  side gutters. Omit them entirely if they would risk crossing a cut band.

## Top Reconnection

Reconnect the left and right tower groups near the top with one high, graceful
castle element above the short upper center no-feature rectangle.

Use a broad production-friendly architectural connector, such as a high arched
stone skybridge, a crenellated parapet bridge, a delicate elevated castle wall,
or a broad balcony/terrace spanning between the upper towers.

The connector should feel structurally part of the castle. It may arch over the
center wall area, but it must not place tiny focal details inside the short
upper red keep-out zone. The short upper keep-out rectangle should remain free
of focal details, with only plain wall/air behind it.

## Negative Instructions

Do not create a new die-line. Do not leave the center lane completely white. Do
not put focal elements in the center red-rectangle areas. Do not make the middle
look faded out, airbrushed, erased, foggy, transparent, or hidden by pale wash.
Do not push side castle clusters, fairies, birds, butterflies, flowers, wings,
vines, roof tips, or tower edges into the safe-margin gutters. Do not allow the
horizontal panel split to cut through any fairy, bird, butterfly, wing, flower,
face, window, lamp, roof tip, flag, or other recognizable motif.

## Output

Return a high-resolution vertical watercolor artwork on white background,
roughly matching the tall proportions of the supplied template. It should be
beautiful alone, but conservative enough that the exact SVG can be overlaid
without cutting through important details.
