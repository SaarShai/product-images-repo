# Prompt V2 - Mask-First Production Workflow

Create only the watercolor castle artwork for the uploaded two-panel template.
Do not redraw, invent, or stylize the production die-line. The exact cut lines,
slot, panel divide, and top contour will be applied later from the SVG.

Reference roles:
- Style reference: watercolor castle style, palette, mood, and elements.
- Raster template: visual explanation of the two-panel layout.
- SVG template: authoritative production geometry, if available.
- Contour examples: examples of how the final vector top contour may follow the
  castle skyline, but do not draw that final contour yourself.

Primary task:
Generate a new whimsical watercolor castle illustration that fits comfortably
inside the safe artwork area of the template. Leave generous blank white margin
near all cut edges, around the central vertical slot, around the horizontal panel
divide, and inside all no-feature zones.

Style:
Match the reference closely: delicate children's-book watercolor, warm cream and
ivory stone, terracotta/coral roof spires, pale gold glowing windows, blue-grey
shadows, peach/blush/yellow accents, muted sage foliage, tiny birds,
butterflies, fairies, flowers, vines, magical glow, and airy white background.

Composition:
Create one continuous tall castle scene that naturally spans the upper and lower
panel areas. Use towers, spires, parapets, ledges, crenellations, windows,
subtle masonry, vines, flowers, birds, butterflies, and a few fairies.

Hard placement rules:
- Keep all focal elements well inside the safe area.
- Keep the central vertical slot completely blank.
- Keep a clear buffer around the central slot.
- Keep the horizontal panel divide free of focal elements.
- Do not place fairies, birds, butterflies, faces, windows, doors, flags, roof
  peaks, spire tips, flowers, or decorative focal motifs in the no-feature
  rectangles.
- Let no-feature rectangles contain only generic pale stone wall texture or
  blank background.
- Keep small flying details away from the outer contour so they do not force a
  fussy cut line.

Top skyline:
Design the upper castle skyline with varied tower heights and spires so a later
SVG/vector contour can trace the major silhouette gracefully. Do not place tiny
details above the main skyline.

Output:
Return clean watercolor artwork on a white background. Do not show dashed guide
lines, labels, annotations, green/yellow/red construction lines, or invented
production cut lines. The production geometry will be added afterward from the
SVG.
