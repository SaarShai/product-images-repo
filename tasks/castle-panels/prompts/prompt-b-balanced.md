# Prompt B - Balanced

Create a new two-panel fairytale castle illustration based on the uploaded
references.

Reference roles:
- Style reference: style and palette.
- Raster template: explanatory panel layout.
- Contour examples: examples of a successfully adapted top contour.
- SVG template: authoritative source for dimensions, cut lines, safe area, slot,
  and panel division when available.

Style:
Match the look of the watercolor castle reference closely: soft watercolor
children's-book illustration, elegant and whimsical, pale cream stone towers,
warm coral / terracotta roofs, glowing yellow windows, soft blue-grey shadows,
airy white space, delicate magical atmosphere, and gentle fairytale detailing.
Include similarly charming elements such as birds, butterflies, fairies, floral
touches, vines, and softly painted architectural details.

Goal:
Create a new illustration that feels like it belongs in the same world as the
watercolor castle reference, while fitting exactly into the uploaded two-panel
template.

Panel structure:
Design one continuous vertical castle scene across a top sub-panel and a bottom
sub-panel. The artwork should feel intentionally composed for this panel format
rather than simply cropped into it.

Geometry:
Use the uploaded SVG as the main structural guide when available. If no SVG is
available, use the raster template as the geometry guide. All important artwork
must stay within the safe area. Nothing important should extend into the margin
between the safe area and the outer cut line. Preserve the two-panel proportions,
central vertical slot, and horizontal divide exactly.

Custom top contour:
Redraw the top contour of the top sub-panel so it follows the top silhouette of
the castle illustration in a smooth, elegant, production-friendly way. Let it
respond to the major silhouette of towers and spires, similar to the example
contour images. It should feel like a custom die-line shaped around the castle
skyline.

Do not make it a generic rounded arch. Do not make it messy or overly detailed.
Do not let tiny incidental elements control the contour.

Exclusion zones:
The areas corresponding to the red-dashed rectangles are protected no-feature
zones. Do not place specific elements there, and do not crop specific elements
there. These areas may contain only generic background texture or non-focal
castle wall surfaces.

Slot rule:
The central vertical slot must remain free of specific content. No fairies,
birds, butterflies, windows, faces, flags, flowers, spire tips, or focal details
should touch or cross it.

Divide rule:
The horizontal divide between the top and bottom sub-panels must not cut through
important elements. Allow that line to pass only through non-focal background
material or architectural areas that can tolerate the split, such as stone walls
or transitional tower sections. Ideally, compose nearby castle details so the
divide feels intentional, for example with ledges, crenellations, roof bases,
stone bands, or lower spire sections that visually relate to the split.

Content:
Create a beautiful whimsical castle composition using similar kinds of elements
to the style reference:
- multiple towers of varying heights
- elegant warm roofs and spires
- glowing windows
- soft stone walls
- subtle magical light
- birds and butterflies in the air
- a few fairies
- some flowers and vegetation near the lower area

Place all specific focal elements safely away from the red exclusion zones, the
slot, the outer margin, and the horizontal divide.

Output:
Return a clean final production-style image with the illustration, the final
black cut-line contour, the panel division, and the central slot. Do not show the
dashed guides or annotations.

Ensure the finished image is both visually beautiful and layout-compliant.
