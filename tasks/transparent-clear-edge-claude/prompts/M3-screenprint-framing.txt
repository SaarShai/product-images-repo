Prepare the artwork like a print-ready color separation with a color hold: every element carries a closed keyline printed in a darker tone of its own ink color (never black, never white), so each shape is fully trapped with no gaps and no bleed into the page. The page itself is unprinted — flat pure white #FFFFFF, no texture, wash, shadow, or glow. Any white-looking detail inside the artwork is printed as a pale tinted ink (cream, blush, pale blue), never left as unprinted paper.
