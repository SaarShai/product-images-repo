# Round2 Option C: Frosted Snowflake Lattice

You are generating a real styled watercolor candidate, not a procedural preview.

Use the first attached image only as a composition and negative-space map. It shows
the exact cutout positions. Do not copy its flat pixels. Repaint the decoration set
as one coherent watercolor illustration using the attached reference images,
especially the snowflake reference (pale icy blue watercolor blooms, soft no-outline
edges) plus sparse candy accents from the ornament and Christmas-elements refs.

Output a single 1400x1899 PNG. Keep the background clean warm cream paper
(~#FCF9F0). Put decoration artwork only inside the visible cutout silhouettes from
the composition map. Leave all areas outside those silhouettes blank paper.

Subject: gingerbread-house candy decorations only. The panels are already the
gingerbread-house roof and walls; do not put houses inside the cutouts.

Motifs (cool powdered-sugar / snowflake lattice):
- roof/top cutout: overlapping soft blue watercolor snowflakes and powdered-sugar
  crystal washes that follow the jagged roof contour, with a few tiny red/green
  candy pinpoints and pale icing drips at the peaks;
- narrow top slot: a delicate vertical trail of sugar crystals and one tiny
  snowflake;
- lower tall slots: stacked soft snowflake marks and icy blue sugar lattice that
  echo the long scalloped shapes, with sparse peppermint buttons as warm accents;
- small round/star holes: single frosted snowflake candies or icy sugar stars.

Style requirements from the attached references:
- visible watercolor paper texture, pigment blooms, uneven edges, transparent
  washes, soft bleed, hand-painted line variation;
- match the snowflake reference for pale icy blue mottled washes and soft
  outline-free edges;
- keep candy accents secondary so the overall read is frosty sugar, not dense
  ornament clutter;
- calm, delicate, wintry handmade feel.

Hard negatives:
- no miniature houses, buildings, windows, doors, facades, chimneys, villages,
  signs, words, labels, or text;
- no flat vector snowflakes, no sterile procedural dots, no crop collage, no UI
  style, no hard rectangular background, no photo-realism;
- do not let any decoration extend outside the cutout silhouettes.
