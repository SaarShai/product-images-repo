# AB5 Bottom-Right Semi-Arched Shape V1: Peppermint Holly

Generate one styled watercolor image for the single semi-arched shape shown in
the first attached image.

Geometry:
- Use the first attached image as the exact composition/geometry guide.
- It is one tall gingerbread-cookie piece with a straight left side, flat
  bottom, and a rounded/arched top-right corner.
- Keep it as one object, centered on warm white watercolor paper.
- Do not crop, clip, or mask away the object's drawn edge. The full object edge,
  drop shadow, icing border, and paper around it should remain visible.

Style target:
- Match the approved gingerbread watercolor style in the other attached
  references: warm hand-painted gingerbread surface, raised white piped icing
  border, soft beige edge shadow, pale icing highlights, watercolor grain,
  slightly uneven hand-painted edges, candy with glossy highlights.

Decoration:
- Gingerbread-house candy decorations only. The panel itself represents a house
  part; do not add a house, window, door, facade, chimney, village, sign, label,
  or text.
- Add a peppermint ribbon/rope that follows the arched top-right contour, then
  sweeps down softly through the tall body.
- Add small holly leaves and red berries near the arch and lower corner, plus
  scattered white sugar pearls and tiny gumdrops.
- Details should sit inside the gingerbread piece and complement the tall
  semi-arched shape.

Hard negatives:
- no miniature houses/buildings/windows/doors/facades/chimneys/villages;
- no text, labels, signs, UI, flat vector look, black/magenta background;
- no cutout-mask/crop look and no decoration extending beyond the object.

Output one high-resolution PNG.
