# AB5 Styled V1: Peppermint Holly Icing Cutouts

You are generating a real styled watercolor candidate, not a procedural preview.

Use the first attached image as the AB5 composition and geometry guide. It shows
six cutout-shaped gingerbread pieces: two roof-like top pieces and four tall
wavy vertical strips. Keep that overall six-piece layout, spacing, and
negative-space structure.

Use the attached AB2 gingerbread cutout artworks as the visual style target:
warm watercolor gingerbread-cookie fill, raised white piped icing border, soft
inner shadow, pale icing highlights, visible watercolor paper grain, and
hand-painted candy details. The result should feel like the same product family
as those AB2 images.

Subject: gingerbread-house candy decorations only. The panels already represent
walls/roof; do not put houses, windows, doors, facades, chimneys, villages,
signs, labels, or text inside the cutouts.

Style and composition:
- All six cutouts must have the same warm gingerbread-cookie background and a
  dimensional white icing border.
- Add watercolor peppermint ropes, soft red-and-white candy ribbons, small
  gumdrops, sugar pearls, and a few holly/berry sprigs.
- Let the candy/icing details follow the contours: arcing along the roof-like
  top pieces and snaking vertically through the tall strips.
- Keep details inside the cutout silhouettes. Leave the surrounding background
  clean warm white paper.
- The art must look hand-painted: pigment blooms, translucent washes, uneven
  edges, soft bleed, little glossy candy highlights, and watercolor texture.

Hard negatives:
- no miniature houses/buildings/windows/doors/facades/chimneys/villages;
- no flat vector icons, no sterile procedural dots, no crop collage, no UI
  style, no black background, no magenta background, no photorealism;
- no decoration extending beyond the six cutout silhouettes.

Output one high-resolution PNG.
