# Styled Redraw V2: Gumdrop Sugar Beads

You are generating a real styled watercolor candidate, not a procedural preview.

Use the first attached image only as a composition and negative-space map. It
shows the exact cutout positions and a rough bead/garland idea. Do not copy its
flat pixels. Repaint the decoration set as one coherent watercolor illustration
using the attached reference images and style packet sheets as the visual style
source.

Output a single 1400x1899 PNG. Keep the background clean warm white paper. Place
artwork only inside the cutout silhouettes shown by the composition map; leave
everything outside those silhouettes blank paper.

Subject: gingerbread-house candy decorations only. The panels themselves are the
gingerbread roof and walls, so the cutouts should contain candy and icing
decorations, not house imagery.

Motifs:
- roof/top cutout: a curved gumdrop garland following the roof contour, with
  red, green, gold, and pale blue candies rendered like watercolor ornaments;
- narrow top slot: tiny stacked sugar beads and a small painted icing trail;
- lower tall slots: bead chains and icing dots that climb the wavy vertical
  contours, with occasional holly-berry accents;
- small holes: small peppermint disks or frosted gumdrop buttons.

Style requirements from the attached references:
- visible watercolor grain, brush pooling, pigment bloom, soft hand-painted
  highlights, and imperfect edges;
- use the ornament reference for rounded candy shading, the Christmas element
  reference for red/green holiday candy and holly, and the snowflake reference
  for pale frosty blue sugar texture;
- charming hand-painted collection-piece feel, not mechanical pattern fill.

Hard negatives:
- no miniature houses, buildings, windows, doors, facades, chimneys, villages,
  signs, words, labels, or text;
- no flat vector icons, no procedural scatter, no crop collage, no UI style, no
  hard rectangular background, no photorealistic candy;
- no decoration crossing outside the cutout silhouettes.
