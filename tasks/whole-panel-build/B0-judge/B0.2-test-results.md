## Reading
- The deterministic layer (B0.1) could NOT separate these; the judge works by **reading the registered
  overlay against the lines** — which is what makes the injected defects (and the real FAIL's defects) visible.
- New tool: `make_anchor_overlay.py --base <candidate.png>` draws the guide lines on ANY candidate (the
  judge's overlay step for real generated images, not just embedded-raster anchors).
