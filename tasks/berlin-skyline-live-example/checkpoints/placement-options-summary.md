## Option A: Conservative Template-Safe

- Safest option for avoiding cropped focal details.
- Keep whole landmarks well inside each panel.
- Let only plain train body, rail, stone, water, or quiet texture cross seams
  and red zones.
- Bridge/viaduct arch sits in the saloon area, but does not dominate.
- Risk: can feel rigid or like separate postcards.

## Option B: Connected Scenic Set

- Best option for a continuous Berlin scene.
- Foreground U-Bahn/rail, middle water/bridge band, background landmarks
  staggered by depth.
- Dom is main central mass, Kaiser Wilhelm tower is the vertical counterpoint,
  and the right hotel includes its lower podium/wing.
- Risk: needs whole-scene redraw or careful compositing to avoid feeling like a
  collage.

## Option C: Saloon Arch + Run-Through

- Best option for making the orange saloon-door arch meaningful.
- Enlarges the bridge/viaduct arch as the hero of the central lower flaps.
- U-Bahn/rail/stone/water become quiet connective infrastructure across all
  panels.
- Risk: bridge can become too busy or too literal; train windows must be kept
  away from seams.

## Shared Placement Rules

- Exclude the green traffic-light/clock tower.
- Preserve full hotel lower podium/wing in the right panel.
- Keep U-Bahn doors/windows, birds, text, bridge towers, Quadriga, dome cross,
  spires, and roof crowns away from seams/red zones.
- Keep sky/paper background white.
- Use real skyline contour later: TV tower rise, gate dip, Dom dome, church
  tower, stepped hotel crown.
