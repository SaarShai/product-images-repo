# Final Image-Generation Prompt

Use the attached images as visual references.

The first two attached control-panel images are rough composition/control maps
only. They show the approximate die-cut silhouette, the large diagonal rounded
slot, the lower center rectangular void, the lower-right round cutout, and a
useful distribution of controls. Do not reuse, copy, trace, composite, crop, or
preserve their pixels as final art.

Create a fresh single polished watercolor illustration of one blue die-cut
control-panel object on a clean white background. Repaint the whole object as a
coherent hand-painted panel, using the style packet/reference sheets as the
style source: friendly rounded geometry, blue ink outlines, soft uneven
watercolor edges, visible paper granulation, pooled shadows under raised
controls, pale highlights from the upper left, and simple toy-like control
parts.

Geometry and negative-space requirements:

- Keep the outer silhouette close to the rough maps: tall rounded upper-left
  shoulder, sloped left side, stepped upper-right shoulder, broad right wing,
  lower left base, lower center rectangular notch/void, and small bottom tabs.
- The large diagonal rounded slot through the right half must remain completely
  empty white background. Do not draw buttons, rails, wires, shadows, texture,
  or hardware across it.
- The lower center rectangular cutout/void must remain empty white background.
  Do not place the dial, buttons, or blue panel wash inside it.
- The lower-right circular cutout must remain empty white background.
- Leave a quiet blue material margin around every cutout so the holes look
  intentional, clean, and unscarred.

Composition:

- Upper-left tall bay: three small raised colored pins or pegs with dark blue
  washer bases, red/yellow/teal, arranged naturally rather than copied exactly.
- Middle-left field: two or three thin horizontal slider rails with tiny square
  colored nubs and watercolor shadowing.
- Lower-left base bay: one large circular dial/gauge partly but safely within
  the material area, plus two or three rounded capsule buttons.
- Lower-middle/right-safe bay: two or three rounded capsule buttons, staggered
  and balanced away from the rectangular void and round cutout.
- Right vertical strip: a few small screw heads, rivets, tick marks, or tiny
  indicator slits placed only in the safe blue material, never inside the round
  cutout or diagonal slot.
- Add subtle believable details that make the object feel designed: faint
  watercolor seams, tiny white tick marks near the dial, small edge highlights,
  softened screw slots, and gentle shadow pools. Keep the density light and
  readable like the references.

Style constraints:

- One unified watercolor object, not a procedural layout, not pasted sprites,
  not a clipped rectangular panel.
- No labels, numbers, text, arrows, yellow dashed guides, template annotations,
  or construction marks.
- No flat vector UI, glossy app controls, photoreal metal, hard 3D render, dark
  machinery, sci-fi grime, or busy circuit-board detail.
- Preserve the white background outside the object and inside all cutouts.

Output a single high-resolution PNG candidate, square-ish aspect ratio matching
the reference maps, with the panel centered and fully visible.
