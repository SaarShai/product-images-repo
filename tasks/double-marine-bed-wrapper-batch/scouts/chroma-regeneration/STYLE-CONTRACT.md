# Frozen style and reference-input contract

Shown and logged before every generation call.

## Style

Professional children's picture-book illustration; hand-painted watercolor;
soft translucent washes; gentle pigment granulation; delicate controlled
edges; muted pastel coral, purple, orange, sage-green and blue palette; soft
ambient light; dense but airy marine detail.

This is flat art, not a product substrate simulation.

## Anti-clause

No photorealism, no 3D render, no felt/fabric/fiber texture, no embroidery, no
digital gradient look, no text, no new or missing objects, no crop/reframe, no
composition change, no recoloring of the artwork.

## Reference inputs

The original 941x1672 image 14 is attached to every engine as the exclusive
composition and style reference. No text-to-image route is allowed. No prior
failed regeneration is attached as a positive reference.
