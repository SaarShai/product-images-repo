This directory is the review inbox mirror for round-7 x4 gate outputs and review packs: compact D5 and junction crops are retained for human inspection, while fullres and composite rasters are regenerable and ignored. Regenerate gate evidence with `scripts/gates/gate_battery.py` from the round-7 x4 final/raw inputs and review packs with `scripts/review_pack.py --final <x4-final> --raw <x4-raw> --out-dir <review-pack> --gate-dir <gate-dir> --n-crops 6 --crop-size 96`; the tracked `gates/*/battery.json` and `review_pack/*/manifest.json` files are the proof.
