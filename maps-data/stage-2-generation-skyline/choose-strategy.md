# Which scout reads best?

Inspect the scout candidates from [[scout|scout]] with a vision review (not a
mechanical PASS). Pick the **single** strategy whose hierarchy, landmark
allocation, and run-through route read best within a few seconds.

- **best scout** — one strategy clearly wins → carry it forward to
  [[polish|polish]] and commit to that route.

If no scout passes — all look pasted, or all violate keep-clear zones the same
way — do not promote any of them; restart the method (more abstract composition
map, or a direct whole-scene route) before spending on polish.

Only one branch leaves this decision: the chosen strategy proceeds to polish.
