# Adapt top contour

The green dashed contour is a **placeholder**. The final top contour should trace
the chosen skyline — rooflines, spires, domes, bridge towers, antennas, or other
major silhouette features — instead of staying a generic arch when the selected
buildings give a better production silhouette.

A landmark may rise above the adapted contour only by a controlled amount
(door-panel domes and spires are a valid precedent). A feature that towers far
above must be reduced or repositioned so the silhouette still reads as
intentional production geometry. Crucially, **do not add tiny fragile details
above the contour** unless they are essential and production-safe — thin
antennas and spindly tips break in the die-cut.
