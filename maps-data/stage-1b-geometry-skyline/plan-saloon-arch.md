# Plan saloon-door arch

The orange dashed arch in the central door panel is a **compositional
opportunity, not a pixel-perfect requirement**. Prefer a feature that naturally
fits inside or stylistically echoes the arch — a bridge span, palace gate,
doorway, archway, statue/column, window bay, or an enlarged building entrance.
Proportions may be exaggerated to make the feature work; the arch is matched
generally, not mathematically. For a span (e.g. a bridge), check whether
aligning it to the middle of the saloon-door flaps improves symmetry without
making the scene feel forced.

If no natural feature exists, **record that decision and keep the arch area
quiet** rather than forcing an awkward motif into the central panel.

Gate: **arch placement is optional/quiet, not forcing awkward architecture**.
