# Allocate landmarks

Assign the landmark roster to the three physical panels. Each physical panel
holds one iconic landmark, or a composite of up to three buildings — but every
landmark must read **whole within a single panel**. Do not split a building,
face, statue, sign, distinctive roofline, or any user-named subject across a
panel boundary or across a blue top/bottom separator.

Only quiet, intentionally continuous infrastructure (bridges, rail lines, roads,
rivers, cables, horizon bands, generic facade texture) may run through panels —
and only when planned as continuous infrastructure, never as an excuse to crop a
focal landmark.

Gate: **no landmark split across a panel boundary**. Next: plan the saloon-door
arch at [[plan-saloon-arch|plan-saloon-arch]]. Workflow rules:
[[skyline-workflow|docs/skyline-template-illustration-workflow.md]].
